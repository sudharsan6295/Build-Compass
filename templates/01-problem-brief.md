## The problem
What is going wrong, for whom, and how do we know it is real? (Evidence: tickets, time lost, errors, complaints.)

## Who is affected
Teams, roles, approximate number of people, how often they hit the problem.

## Today's workaround
What people do now, and what it costs in time, errors or money.

## Cost of doing nothing for 12 months
A rough estimate is fine. State your assumptions.

## Existing tools already touching this
Check the AI app register and the application portfolio. List anything bought, subscribed, outsourced or built.

## AI question (keep these separate)
- Does the solution need AI **inside** it? Yes / No / Unsure, and why
- Would we use AI to **build** it? Yes / No

- [ ] Problem stated in business terms, without naming a solution
- [ ] Business sponsor named and committed
