# Vendor contract checklist (Build Compass · buy track)

> Use before signing or renewing. Buying is a decision to own a relationship instead of a codebase.

## Exit and data
- [ ] Full data export in an open, documented format, at any time, at no extra cost
- [ ] Exit assistance period and support defined (duration, cost)
- [ ] Data deletion on exit, with written confirmation

## Price and term
- [ ] Cap on yearly price rises (e.g. CPI or a fixed %)
- [ ] Notice period and renewal date in the calendar, with a review 6 months before
- [ ] Seat true-up rules: can we reduce licences as well as add them?

## Service
- [ ] SLAs for availability and support response, with service credits
- [ ] Incident notification time for security breaches
- [ ] Named account and technical contacts

## Security and AI
- [ ] Certifications in scope (e.g. ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2) and latest reports shared
- [ ] Data residency meets our requirements
- [ ] How the vendor uses our data in its own AI features: training, retention, opt-out, in writing
- [ ] Right to audit or to receive penetration-test summaries

## Roadmap and fit
- [ ] Any must-have gap committed in writing, with a date and a remedy if missed
- [ ] Supported APIs for integration; no unsupported customisation of the core
- [ ] SSO and role-based access supported
